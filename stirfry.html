Stir Fry Recipe

Below you will find the recipe and steps that are needed to create this High Protien Stir Fry! Hope you enjoy it!

Ingredients

Sauce

    1/2 cup low-sodium soy sauce
    2 tablespoons honey
    2 tablespoons gochujang (hot peppers from korea)
    2 teaspoons of sesame oil
    4 cloves garlic, minced

Stir Fry

    6 tablespoons vegatable oil, divied
    16 cremini oinions, white and greeen parts seperated, thinly sliced
    6 carrots, peeled and thinly sliced diagonally
    2 pounds lean ground turkey
    salt and freshly ground black pepper to taste

Directions

    Step 1

    Gather All the Ingredients (Because how else are you going to make the food! Can't forget that!)

    Step 2

    Whisk together soy sauce, honey, gochujang, sesame oil, and garlic in a small bowl; set aside.

    Step 3

    Heat 1 tablespoon vegatble oil in wok or large skillet over medium-high heat. Add mushrooms, white parts of green onions, carrots, and celery. Stir-fry veggies, stirring constantly, until crisp-tender, about 4 to 6 minutes. Transfer to a bowl.

    Step 4

    Heat remaining 2 tablespoons vegatble oil in the wok. Add ground turkey and cook, stiring every 15 to 20 seconds, and break up any large chunks until everly browned, about 5 to 6 minutes.

    Step 5

    Return veggies to the wok and pour sauce over top. Stir to combine and simmer for 2 to 3 minutes.

    Step 6

    Top with green parts from the green onion and season to taste with salt and pepper.
